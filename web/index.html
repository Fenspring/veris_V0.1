You already have the knowledge.
It has never been connected.

Every hospital already holds the regulations, accreditation standards, policies, procedures, education and competencies it needs. Each one is written carefully. Each one is read alone. The organization can be misaligned while every document in it is correct.

Veris does not replace any of it. It is the intelligence layer that connects what you already own — and tells you what those connections mean: what changed, what it affects, where the gaps and conflicts are, and what deserves review.

The hospital owns the knowledge. Veris owns the connections.
Findings are evidence-backed and scoped. Veris recommends human review; it does not determine regulatory compliance.
