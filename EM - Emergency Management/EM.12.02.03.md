# EM.12.02.03

The hospital has a staffing plan for managing all staff and volunteers during an emergency or disaster incident.
Note: The hospital considers its prioritized hazards identified as part of its hazard vulnerability analysis when developing a staffing plan.

## Introduction


When the hospital activates its emergency operations plan in response to an emergency or a disaster, it may find that it is unable to meet or maintain the immediate needs of patients. An emergency staffing plan focuses on strategies for quickly and effectively managing all staff and volunteers who may be needed during an incident response. During an emergency or disaster incident, hospitals should anticipate staffing needs, such as shortages, and prepare to obtain staff from other sources, such as a staffing agency or disaster medical assistance teams.

Testing health care staffing needs during emergency management exercises allows participants to identify gaps or weaknesses and provides opportunities to make improvements. The hospital’s own staff can become overwhelmed, exhausted, or depleted during extended emergency or disaster incidents. Failure to recognize and develop an emergency staffing plan may result in an inability to provide the necessary services. (For testing requirements, refer to Standard EM.16.01.01

## Rationale

The hospital’s emergency staffing plan should be activated in response to an emergency or disaster when the hospital is unable to meet or maintain the immediate or ongoing needs of its patients. Hospitals that anticipate staffing shortages, based on their prioritized risks, are better prepared to respond to and quickly obtain staff from within their health care system or from staffing agencies or by integrating volunteer licensed practitioners or those who are federally deployed as part of disaster medical assistance teams. Hospitals should stand ready to adjust their staffing models to meet the needs of their staff; for example, supporting staff’s emotional and mental health needs that may arise during an emergency or disaster.  

## Elements of Performance

### EP 1

The hospital develops a staffing plan for managing all staff and volunteers to meet patient care needs during the duration of an emergency or disaster incident or during a patient surge. The plan includes the following:  
- Methods for contacting off-duty staff
- Acquisition of staff from its other health care facilities 
- Use of volunteer staffing, such as staffing agencies, health care coalition support, and those deployed as part of the disaster medical assistance teams  
Note: If the hospital determines that it will never use volunteers during disasters, this is documented in its plan.

**Attributes:** Documentation, ESP-1

**Crosswalk:** §482.15(b)(6), §482.15(b)(6)

### EP 2

The hospital's staffing plan addresses the management of all staff and volunteers as follows:
- Reporting processes
- Roles and responsibilities for essential functions
- Integration of staffing agencies, volunteer staffing, or deployed medical assistance teams into assigned roles and responsibilities

**Attributes:** ESP-1, Documentation
