# Controlled Substance Safety — Annual Education Module

Required annually for all licensed staff who administer controlled substances.

## §1 Learning Objective — Two-Person Waste

The learner will describe why controlled substance waste requires a second licensed
witness, and will state that the witness must directly observe the volume discarded
rather than attest to it afterwards.

## §2 Learning Objective — Documentation

The learner will demonstrate documenting waste in the automated dispensing cabinet,
including amount wasted, reason, and witness identity.

## §3 Learning Objective — Discrepancy Response

The learner will state that count discrepancies must be reconciled within 24 hours
and will describe the escalation pathway to pharmacy and the nursing supervisor.

## §4 Learning Objective — Diversion Awareness

The learner will identify behavioural and documentation indicators that may suggest
drug diversion, and will state the non-punitive reporting pathway.
