# NS-CS.02.01 — Controlled Substance Waste and Reconciliation

The organization manages controlled substances so that every dose procured,
administered, wasted, and returned can be accounted for.

## Elements of Performance

### EP 1

The organization has written policies and procedures governing the procurement, storage, administration, wasting, and return of controlled substances.

**Attributes:** Documentation

### EP 2

Waste of any controlled substance is witnessed by a second licensed person and documented.

**Attributes:** Documentation

### EP 3

Discrepancies in controlled substance counts are investigated and reconciled within 24 hours of identification.

**Attributes:** Documentation

### EP 4

Staff who administer controlled substances receive education on controlled substance handling at orientation and annually thereafter.

**Attributes:** Documentation

### EP 5

Competency for controlled substance handling is validated for licensed staff at orientation.

**Attributes:** Documentation
