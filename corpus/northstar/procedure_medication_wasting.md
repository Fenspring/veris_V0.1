# Medication Wasting Procedure

Step-level procedure for wasting unused controlled substance doses on nursing units.

## §1 Prepare the Waste

Draw up only the ordered dose. Retain the remaining volume in the syringe or vial
until a witness is available. Do not discard any portion before the witness observes it.

## §2 Obtain a Witness

Any licensed staff member present on the unit may serve as the waste witness,
including the nurse who administered the dose. The witness must directly observe
the volume discarded.

## §3 Discard the Waste

Discard the unused portion into the pharmaceutical waste container. Never discard
controlled substances into a sharps container or a sink.

## §4 Document the Waste

Record the waste in the automated dispensing cabinet before the end of the shift in
which the dose was administered. Enter the amount wasted, the reason, and the witness
name.

## §5 Resolve Discrepancies

If the cabinet reports a discrepancy, notify the charge nurse and pharmacy. Complete
the discrepancy resolution form before leaving the unit.
