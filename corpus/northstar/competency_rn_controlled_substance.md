# RN Controlled Substance Handling Competency

Validated at orientation and upon change of practice setting. Method: direct
observation with return demonstration.

## §1 Criterion — Retrieves Dose Correctly

The RN retrieves only the ordered dose from the automated dispensing cabinet and
verifies the count displayed against the physical count.

## §2 Criterion — Wastes With a Witness

The RN obtains a second licensed witness before discarding any unused portion, and
the witness observes the discard directly.

## §3 Criterion — Documents Waste

The RN records the waste in the automated dispensing cabinet, entering amount, reason,
and witness.

## §4 Criterion — Responds to Discrepancy

The RN describes the escalation pathway when the cabinet reports a count discrepancy.
