# Controlled Substance Inventory Count Procedure

Governs periodic physical inventory of controlled substances held in the pharmacy vault.

## §1 Biennial Inventory

A complete physical inventory of all controlled substances in the vault is conducted
every two years and retained for the period required by law.

## §2 Perpetual Inventory Records

Pharmacy maintains a perpetual inventory for Schedule II substances. Each receipt and
each issue to a unit is posted the same day.

## §3 Vault Access Log

Entry to the controlled substance vault is logged. The log records the individual,
date, time, and purpose of entry.
