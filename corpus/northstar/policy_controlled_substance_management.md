# Controlled Substance Management Policy

Northstar Health accounts for every controlled substance dose from receipt through
final disposition.

## §1 Two-Person Waste Verification

Any unused portion of a controlled substance must be wasted in the presence of a
second licensed person, who verifies the amount discarded. Both individuals record
the waste. Waste must never be deferred to another staff member who did not observe it.

## §2 Count Discrepancy Reconciliation

Discrepancies identified during shift count must be investigated and reconciled within
24 hours. Unresolved discrepancies are escalated to the Director of Pharmacy and the
nursing supervisor.

## §3 Secure Storage

Controlled substances are stored in automated dispensing cabinets under double-lock
control. Access is limited to licensed staff with an active assignment on the unit.

## §4 Procurement and Receipt

Controlled substances are received by pharmacy personnel only. Receipt is verified
against the purchase record and entered into the perpetual inventory on the day of
delivery.
