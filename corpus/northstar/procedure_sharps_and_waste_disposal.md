# Sharps and Regulated Waste Disposal Procedure

Governs disposal of sharps and regulated medical waste.

## §1 Sharps Containers

Sharps are discarded immediately at the point of use into an approved puncture-resistant
container. Containers are replaced when filled to the marked line.

## §2 Regulated Medical Waste Segregation

Regulated medical waste is segregated at the point of generation into red bagged
containers. Waste is weighed and manifested before transport by the licensed hauler.

## §3 Waste Stream Documentation

The waste manifest is retained for three years. Quantities of waste removed are recorded
by stream and reconciled against hauler invoices monthly.
