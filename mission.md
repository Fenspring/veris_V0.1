# VERIS MISSION

## The Mission

Veris exists to help healthcare organizations understand their knowledge as one connected system.

Healthcare organizations already possess the knowledge they need.

That knowledge may exist across:

* Regulatory standards
* Accreditation standards
* Government requirements
* State requirements
* Organizational policies
* Procedures
* Education
* Competencies
* Clinical guidelines
* Quality resources
* Patient-safety resources
* Department documentation
* Other organizational knowledge

Veris does not need to own that knowledge.

The hospital already has it.

The hospital may already license it.

The hospital may already store it in systems it already uses.

**Veris is the intelligence layer that connects it.**

---

## The Core Problem

Healthcare knowledge is fragmented.

Each knowledge source can be individually correct while the organization as a whole is still:

* Misaligned
* Inconsistent
* Missing important relationships
* Duplicative
* Outdated in places
* Difficult to understand
* Difficult to maintain
* Vulnerable to changes propagating through one part of the organization without being recognized elsewhere

The problem is not primarily information retrieval.

The problem is **relationship understanding**.

---

## The Core Idea

Veris should understand the semantic relationships between knowledge.

A regulatory requirement may relate to a policy.

A policy may relate to a procedure.

A procedure may relate to education.

Education may relate to a competency.

A competency may relate to a role.

A requirement may relate to all of them.

The organization may be unable to see these relationships when each system is examined independently.

**Veris exists to make those relationships visible and useful.**

---

## The Product

Veris is a semantic connection and intelligence layer.

The hospital provides the knowledge.

Veris:

1. Understands the knowledge.
2. Identifies meaningful concepts.
3. Identifies semantic relationships.
4. Connects related knowledge.
5. Detects potential gaps.
6. Detects potential conflicts.
7. Identifies dependencies.
8. Identifies changes and potential downstream impact.
9. Explains its reasoning using evidence.
10. Gives humans a clearer understanding of the organization.

---

## The Most Important Principle

> **The hospital owns the knowledge. Veris owns the connections.**

This is the foundational product concept.

Veris does not need to become the authoritative repository for every regulatory standard, policy, procedure, education file, or organizational document.

Its value comes from understanding what those things mean **in relation to one another**.

---

## The Intelligence

The desired output is not simply:

> "Here is the document that answers your question."

The desired output is:

> "Here is what these pieces of knowledge mean together."

For example:

A hospital may have:

**Requirement**
→ Requires medication safety controls.

**Policy**
→ Requires barcode medication administration.

**Education**
→ Teaches barcode medication administration.

**Competency**
→ Does not explicitly address barcode medication administration.

Individually, each artifact may look reasonable.

Together, Veris may identify:

> Potential alignment gap: the organization's policy and education address barcode medication administration, but the competency framework does not appear to explicitly validate the same requirement.

That is the type of insight Veris should produce.

---

## The North Star

Veris should continually move toward answering:

> **What does this knowledge mean in relation to everything else we know?**

And, eventually:

> **What changed, what does it affect, where are we aligned, where are we not, and what deserves human attention?**

---

## What Veris Is Not

Do not define Veris primarily as:

* A chatbot
* A document repository
* A regulatory database
* A policy management system
* An LMS
* A generic RAG application
* A search engine

Those technologies or interfaces may exist inside Veris.

They are not the product thesis.

---

## The Long-Term Vision

Veris should become the semantic intelligence layer across a healthcare organization's knowledge ecosystem.

The user should be able to look at one piece of knowledge and understand:

* What it connects to
* Why it connects
* What supports the relationship
* What depends on it
* What may conflict with it
* What appears to be missing
* What changed
* What could be affected
* What requires review

The ultimate goal is not to create more information.

It is to make existing information **intelligent when connected**.

---

## The Standard

When evaluating a feature, architecture, or product decision, ask:

> Does this help Veris understand relationships between knowledge that humans currently struggle to see?

If yes, it may belong.

If it merely adds another disconnected feature, reconsider it.
