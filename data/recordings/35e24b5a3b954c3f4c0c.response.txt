Controlled substance handling education is delivered through an annual module whose objectives include describing why waste requires a second licensed witness and that the witness must directly observe the volume discarded rather than attest to it afterwards [E4]. The accreditation standard requires that staff who administer controlled substances receive education on controlled substance handling at orientation and annually thereafter [E6]. Alongside the education module, the RN competency validates that staff can describe the escalation pathway when the automated dispensing cabinet reports a count discrepancy [E5].
