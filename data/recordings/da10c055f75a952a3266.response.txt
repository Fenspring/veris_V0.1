Any unused portion of a controlled substance must be wasted in the presence of a second licensed person who verifies the amount discarded, and both individuals record the waste [E2]. The accreditation standard in force requires that the witness be a second licensed person who did not administer the dose, and that the waste be documented in the automated dispensing cabinet at the time it occurs rather than retrospectively [E1]. The nursing procedure directs staff to record the waste in the cabinet before the end of the shift in which the dose was administered, entering the amount wasted, the reason and the witness name [E3]. Staff are taught to document waste in the cabinet including amount, reason and witness identity [E4], and RNs are validated on doing so as a competency criterion [E5].
