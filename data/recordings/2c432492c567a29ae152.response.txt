Perform hand hygiene immediately before any invasive procedure or sterile technique and immediately before donning sterile gloves [S5]. Assess the central line dressing against its schedule and change it immediately if it is soiled, loose, lifting, bloody or non-occlusive, regardless of when it is next due, confirming a physician order or standing protocol first [S4]. Open the dressing kit and supplies onto a clean dry surface using aseptic non-touch technique, and do not contaminate the inner sterile field or touch sterile surfaces with ungloved hands [S3]. Scrub the insertion site and surrounding skin with a 2% chlorhexidine applicator in a back-and-forth motion for at least 30 seconds over a 3-4 cm radius, then let it air dry completely for a minimum of 30 seconds without fanning, blowing or blotting, as the chlorhexidine must be fully dry before the dressing is applied; use a 70% isopropyl alcohol scrub if chlorhexidine is contraindicated [S2]. Ongoing surveillance of healthcare-associated infections is conducted and reportable conditions are submitted to public health authorities within required timeframes [S1].
